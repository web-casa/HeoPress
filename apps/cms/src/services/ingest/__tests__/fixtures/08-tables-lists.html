Comparing storage plans

Small teams usually need shared folders, version history and simple access control. The table below compares three typical plans on these points and on price per user.

PlanStorageHistory
Basic100 GB30 days
Team1 TB180 days
Business5 TB1 year
  • Basic suits freelancers with few shared files.
  • Team adds admin controls and audit logs.
  • Business adds retention rules for regulated industries.

Whatever the plan, check how easy it is to export everything if you ever need to move to another provider.
