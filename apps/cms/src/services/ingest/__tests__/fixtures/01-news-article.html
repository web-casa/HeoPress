Council approves cycling network

By Jane Doe

City planners in Example Town approved a new cycling network on Tuesday, adding forty kilometres of protected lanes over the next three years.

The plan, drafted after two years of public consultation, links the railway station with the university campus and the riverside business district.

Officials said the first segment, along Harbour Road, will open next spring, while the remaining routes depend on a regional grant that is still under review.

Local shop owners were divided: some welcomed the extra foot traffic, others worried about losing parking spaces in front of their stores.

The council will publish detailed maps and a construction timetable on its website before the end of the month.
