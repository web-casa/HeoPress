Recipe: lemon cake

A simple lemon cake needs flour, sugar, butter, eggs and two unwaxed lemons. Start by creaming the butter and sugar until pale and fluffy, which takes about five minutes with a mixer.

Beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and the zest of both lemons. Click here for the glaze.

Bake at 180 degrees for forty minutes, or until a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean. Let the cake cool in the tin for ten minutes before turning it out.

For the glaze, mix icing sugar with the juice of one lemon and pour it over the warm cake so it soaks in. Glaze variations.
