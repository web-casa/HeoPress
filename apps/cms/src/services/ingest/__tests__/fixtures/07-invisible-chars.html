How to repot a houseplant

Repot​ a plant when roots grow‍ through the drainage holes or circle the bottom of the pot. Spring is the best time, because the plant is entering its growing season.

Choose a new pot only two to five centimetres wider⁠ than the old one, with drainage holes. Too large a pot holds extra water and can rot the roots.﻿

Water the plant a day before, ease it out, loosen the roots gently, and set it at the same depth in fresh potting mix.󠁉󠁇󠁎
